About New Horizons Constructions Co.

Leading Commercial & Industrial Construction Excellence

Industry Leaders in Construction

New Horizons Constructions Co. stands as California's premier general contractor, specializing in large-scale commercial and industrial construction projects. With over 25 years of proven excellence, we transform ambitious visions into structural realities.
