About New Horizons Constructions

Excellence in Construction Since 1999

Who We Are

New Horizons Constructions Co. is a premier general contractor with over 25 years of excellence in commercial and industrial construction. Based in La Puente, California, we've become synonymous with quality, innovation, and reliability in the construction industry.

Our team of 500+ dedicated professionals brings expertise, passion, and commitment to every project we undertake. From concept to completion, we ensure that every structure we build exceeds expectations and stands as a testament to our dedication to excellence.

We specialize in large-scale commercial developments, industrial facilities, and mixed-use projects that transform communities and drive business success. Our portfolio includes work for Fortune 500 companies, government agencies, and private developers across California and beyond.
